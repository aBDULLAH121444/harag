# **App Name**: Haraj Al-Yemen

## Core Features:

- Car Listing Creation: Allow sellers to create and publish detailed car listings, including specifications, price, and multiple images.
- Search & Filter Listings: Enable buyers to search for cars and apply filters based on make, model, year, price range, location, and other relevant attributes.
- User Authentication & Profiles: Users can register, log in, and manage their personal profiles, including saved searches and their own posted listings.
- Listing Management: Sellers can view, edit, mark as sold, or delete their active car listings from their dashboard.
- Smart Description Assistant: An AI tool that assists sellers in generating compelling and descriptive car advertisements based on key details provided.
- Direct Contact for Buyers: Provide a simple and secure method for interested buyers to contact sellers directly regarding their listings.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep, sophisticated forest green (#2C574B) to convey reliability, sturdiness, and a grounded marketplace feel.
- Background color: A very light, almost white tone with a hint of cool green (#F4F8F7) for a clean, accessible, and modern canvas.
- Accent color: A vibrant, clear sky blue (#26B2DA) to provide energetic highlights, draw attention to call-to-actions, and create visual interest.
- Headline and body font: 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif for its modern, machined, and highly legible appearance across all text.
- Utilize clean, intuitive line icons that represent common automotive features and marketplace actions (e.g., car types, search, message, edit).
- Implement a clear, card-based layout for car listings to ensure high readability and scannability, with intuitive navigation menus.
- Incorporate subtle hover effects on listings and interactive elements, and smooth transitions when applying filters or navigating between pages to enhance user experience.
